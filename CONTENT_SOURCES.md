# Curated video sources

The archive links to official CarryMinati or CarryisLive channel uploads; it does not rehost video. Titles and video IDs are centralized in [`src/data/videos.ts`](src/data/videos.ts), and every entry links to its original YouTube page. The twelve added entries were identified in the official channel video listings and their exact public titles/uploader attribution were cross-checked with YouTube oEmbed. Individual collaborators are named only when explicitly credited in a title; group appearances without named credits remain general.
